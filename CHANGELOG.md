# Buff Sentinel

## 1.4.0
- Alt+Right-click a bar to hide it: its group is no longer tracked (When to track: Off). `/bsent undo` brings
  the last one back.

## 1.3.0
- Renamed from BuffWatch to Buff Sentinel (folder `BuffSentinel`, command `/bsent`).
- Click a bar to fix it (out of combat): casts your own buff on you (armors, Inner Fire, Aspects, tracking,
  your class's blessing or Fortitude), uses the sleeping bag or Elemental Attractor / Arcane Censer, equips the
  Argent Dawn Commission, or puts the poison or oil that was on a weapon back on. Hover a bar to see what a click
  does. Option: "Click a bar to fix it".
- Argent Dawn: reminds you to equip the Argent Dawn Commission (or Seal / Rune of the Dawn) in Stratholme,
  Scholomance and the Plaguelands.
- Paladin auras are tracked; they're only reported missing when a paladin is in your own party (in a raid: your
  raid group), as auras only reach the paladin's party.
- Offline group members no longer count as someone who can buff you.
